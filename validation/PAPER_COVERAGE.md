This index follows the 18 November 2026 version of *An exact spectral construction for the optimal planar torsion gradient*. The objects in [SubmissionStatement.lean](../SubmissionStatement.lean) are the weak Dirichlet solution, the essential supremum of its weak gradient, planar Lebesgue measure, and the Cauchy image of the second restricted eigenfunction. The [aggregate proof](../Solution.lean) proves both main theorems with those definitions.

## Proof routes and numerical precision

The spectral profile is constructed on the interval. The proof of boundary attainment uses a [harmonic comparison on a rectangle](../Torsion/Proof/PaperRectangleComparison.lean). The classification uses compactness and the calibrated deficit. These arguments supply the conclusions for which the manuscript appeals to results of other authors.

For each number of modes the Ritz bound is exact. In particular the rational four-mode vector `(100000, −24386, 1191, −220)` proves `secondEigenvalue < 2.754761863`. The Galerkin and Poisson tables record numerical experiments. Their six-place agreement for the sharp constant is empirical.
